## 1. App name (title)

Partner Dashboard field: **App name**
Character limit: 30 characters strict (Shopify enforces this hard)

### Current (8 chars)
```
AI Catalog Score
```

### New (30 chars exactly)
```
AI Catalog Score: ChatGPT SEO
```

Rationale: brings the searchable terms (ChatGPT, SEO) into the indexed title without exceeding the limit. Falls back to "AI Catalog Score" in shorter UI surfaces because the colon separator means Shopify often truncates after it.

If 30 chars is the hard limit and you prefer a different keyword mix, alternates of similar length:

- `AI Catalog Score for ChatGPT` (28 chars)
- `AI Catalog Audit for ChatGPT` (28 chars)
- `ChatGPT Catalog Score: AI SEO` (29 chars)

Pick the one that feels most aligned with how you want the app to be cited (the recommended version keeps your brand name "AI Catalog Score" front-loaded).

## 9. After saving

Shopify re-indexes the listing every 24 to 48 hours. Don't expect immediate ranking changes. Check rank for the keywords above on day 3, day 7, day 14.

To track:
- Search Shopify App Store for "AI catalog" → check your position
- Search "ChatGPT SEO" → check your position
- Search "AI shopping" → check your position
- Search "metafields AI" → check your position

If you're not in the top 20 results for at least one of these after 14 days, the title/tagline needs another iteration.

## 10. Conversion levers (not ASO but related)

ASO drives impressions. Conversion (install rate from impressions) depends on:

1. **First 3 reviews** — install rate goes from ~5% to ~15-25% once you have 3+ reviews. Get them: ask the first 5 friendly installers via in-app prompt after they've used the app and seen real value.
2. **Featured image** — see point 7. Visual hierarchy matters.
3. **Free plan visibility** — "Free up to 50 SKUs, no credit card" should be visible in the listing description AND in the screenshots.
4. **Built for Shopify badge** — you already have it. Don't lose it (it requires maintaining response times to merchant support and not breaking any policy).
